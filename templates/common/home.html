LEGAL FRAMEWORK

Built around Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019

Every workflow in this platform traces back to a specific statutory obligation — from Section 18 registration duties to Part VIII enforcement powers — so officers act on a defensible, auditable record, and entities always know where they stand.
